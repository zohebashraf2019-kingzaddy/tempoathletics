# TempoAthletics — Brand Story & Vision (draft for editing)

## The name
**Tempo** means two things to a runner:
1. **A tempo run** — the "comfortably hard" pace you can hold when you're honest with yourself. Not a sprint, not a jog.
2. **The tempo of the music** — the beat in your headphones that keeps your feet moving.

TempoAthletics lives where those two meet: the miles where your music and your stride line up.

## Tagline
**Catch up. Find your rhythm.**

## Story
Tempo started with a pair of headphones and a run that finally didn't feel like a chore.

A tempo run is the pace you can hold when you're being honest with yourself. Tempo is also the beat in your ears that keeps your feet moving. We built TempoAthletics around both: clothes for the miles where the music and your stride line up.

Our runner wears headphones because most of us do. He's a little cartoonish because running shouldn't take itself too seriously. And he's always mid-stride, because the whole point is to keep going.

We release in drops. Each one is small and considered, made to be worn on the run and long after it, from the track to the coffee line.

## Vision
Make running feel like your favorite song. A community of runners who move to their own beat, built one drop at a time.

## What we believe
- **Every pace counts.** There's no wrong tempo. The six-minute miler and the first-5K runner get the same gear and the same respect.
- **Rhythm over rush.** Consistency beats intensity. Show up, press play, find the beat, and do it again tomorrow.
- **Run, then live in it.** Mesh where you need air, clean lines everywhere else. Pieces that work at mile four and at brunch after.
